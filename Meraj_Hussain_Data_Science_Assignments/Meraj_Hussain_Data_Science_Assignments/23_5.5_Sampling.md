# 5.5 Sampling
**Student:** Meraj Hussain | **Course:** Data Science

## Task 1: Population and sample examples
**Population examples:** all users of Instagram; all orders placed on Zomato in a month; all students in a college.  
**Sample examples:** 100 selected Instagram users; 200 selected Zomato orders; 40 students chosen for a survey.

## Task 2: Survey restaurant ratings in Ahmedabad
Zomato could make a list of restaurants in Ahmedabad and randomly select 10 restaurants from that list. This is simple random sampling. Each restaurant on the list has an equal chance of selection, which helps reduce selection bias.

## Task 3: Flipkart survey about a new feature
A survey only sent to frequent buyers may miss the views of occasional buyers. To reduce bias, select users from different purchase-frequency groups and randomly sample within each group. This is stratified sampling.

## Task 4: Select 20 users from a daily app
For Instagram, I would divide users into groups based on how often they use the app (daily, weekly, and occasional) and randomly select users from each group until I have 20 people. This helps include different kinds of users.
