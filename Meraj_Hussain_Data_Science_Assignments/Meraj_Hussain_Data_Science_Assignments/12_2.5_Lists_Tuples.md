## Task 5: List of IPL teams and list-versus-tuple note
```python
ipl_teams = ["Chennai Super Kings", "Mumbai Indians", "Rajasthan Royals",
             "Kolkata Knight Riders", "Sunrisers Hyderabad"]
print(ipl_teams)
```
Use a list when the collection may change, such as adding a new team. Use a tuple when the values should remain fixed.
